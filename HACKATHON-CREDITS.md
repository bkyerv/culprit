## 1. Agents for Humans — request first

### Credits and claim mechanism

- Registered participants may request **$50 in AWS Promotional Credits**, while supplies last.
- Sequence: join the hackathon on Devpost → have or create an AWS account → submit the [official credit form](https://forms.gle/Ssr8zLw4afKg114M7).
- Request deadline: **September 11, 2026 at 12:00 PM PT**.
- Credits expire **October 31, 2026** and can be used for most AWS services, including those needed for Strands. Usage beyond the credit is the entrant's responsibility.
- An AWS Builder ID is also needed for the final submission; it is distinct from the AWS account.

Sources: [official rules](https://agentsforhumans.devpost.com/rules), [official FAQ](https://agentsforhumans.devpost.com/details/faqs), [official resources](https://agentsforhumans.devpost.com/resources).

### Kazakhstan eligibility

Kazakhstan is not in the official exclusion list, so a Kazakhstan resident appears geographically eligible if they are above the age of majority and satisfy the other conflict/sanctions conditions. This is an inference from the [official eligibility rules](https://agentsforhumans.devpost.com/rules), not a sponsor eligibility ruling.

### Critical build requirements

- Build a **new** agent during August 10–September 14, 2026 using the **Strands Agents SDK**.
- It must handle a real repetitive task end-to-end, not merely chat about it, in the Everyday, Professional, or Good Neighbor track.
- Amazon Bedrock AgentCore is optional but strengthens the Technical Implementation score.
- Submission deadline: **September 14, 2026 at 5:00 PM PT**.
- Submit a text description, public GitHub/GitLab/Bitbucket repository with a visible MIT or Apache license and README, architecture diagram, public YouTube/Vimeo demo of at most five minutes, and AWS Builder ID. A live demo is optional but can improve the score.
- AI coding assistants are allowed; incorporated pre-existing work must be disclosed.

## 2. CALL-E — request calls immediately

### Credits and claim mechanism

- A new CALL-E account receives **20 free calls**.
- An existing CALL-E account may request **200 additional calls** through the [official request form](https://forms.gle/EPQttEZ1rkW8iq9q6).
- Additional calls are discretionary, while supplies last, and are generally processed in **1–5 business days**. The live request form sets a deadline of **September 14, 2026 at 12:00 PM SGT**.
- Exhausting the allocation pauses access; it does **not** automatically charge the account. Buying more calls is optional.
- Devpost registration is required to enter the hackathon. The rules present the call allocation under the entry procedure but do not explicitly state that the 200-call form itself is limited to registered participants.

Sources: [official rules](https://call-e.devpost.com/rules), [official resources](https://call-e.devpost.com/resources), [official CALL-E integrations repository](https://github.com/CALLE-AI/call-e-integrations).

### Kazakhstan eligibility and product limitation

Kazakhstan is not named in the contest exclusion list, so the user appears geographically eligible subject to age-of-majority and the other general conditions. However, **Kazakhstan is not currently listed as a supported call destination** in CALL-E's official repository. A Kazakhstan-based entrant can compete, but the demo must call one of CALL-E's supported destination countries unless the sponsor enables another region.

### Critical build requirements

- Build a functional app using CALL-E's API, Python/TypeScript SDK, Skill, or MCP for a specific impactful phone-work use case.
- New projects qualify; pre-existing projects must be significantly updated during the submission period and the changes explained.
- Open a pull request to the [official Awesome Phone Call Agents repository](https://github.com/CALLE-AI/awesome-phone-call-agents) in the correct contribution area and submit that PR URL to Devpost.
- Submit a feature description, a public YouTube/Vimeo demo under three minutes, and the email attached to the CALL-E account. A live demo URL is optional, although the working project must be available free for judging/testing.
- Submission deadline: **September 14, 2026 at 11:45 PM SGT**.

## 3. RevenueCat Shipaton 2026 — the largest resource bundle

### How the Ship Kit unlocks

1. Join Shipaton on Devpost.
2. Complete the participant form sent by email after registration.
3. Registration-level perks are emailed immediately.
4. More perks unlock automatically at these milestones: RevenueCat project created, first test purchase, first real Store API call, and first real purchase.

RevenueCat says entrants may unlock up to 25 perks. Sponsor-specific limits and requirements can change, and the rules provide no universal cash-equivalent guarantee. Sources: [official resources and Ship Kit](https://revenuecat-shipaton-2026.devpost.com/resources), [official rules](https://revenuecat-shipaton-2026.devpost.com/rules).

### Credit/free-usage highlights

Available after registration plus the participant form:

- 200 Emergent credits, stated value **$40**.
- 2,000 lim.run credits, stated value **$100**.
- **$10 OpenRouter credit**.
- OneSignal Growth Plan free for up to three months.
- JetBrains Junie free for two months, limited to **200 redemptions**.
- Lance free for three months, stated value **$180**.
- Tenjin Plan S free for three months, stated value **$600**.
- Layers free for two months; Argent free access; Codemagic's 500-build-minute/month free tier.
- **$1,000 Noise matching credits** requires matching spend dollar-for-dollar; it is not $1,000 of unconditional free usage.
- The resources page also advertises **$250 in Stripe credits**; exact redemption conditions arrive with the perk.

After the first RevenueCat test purchase:

- **$100 Sentry credit**.

After connecting a real app store:

- Airbridge GO free for two months, limited to **100 redemptions**, and Airbridge Core free for six months (stated value **$240**).

Custom milestone:

- **$250 Stripe Projects credit** auto-unlocks when the entrant provisions RevenueCat/the project through Stripe Projects and makes a real purchase by **September 30**.

### Kazakhstan eligibility

Kazakhstan is not named in the official exclusion list, so a Kazakhstan resident appears geographically eligible if above the age of majority and otherwise compliant. The finished app must be accessible for download in the United States.

### Critical build requirements

- Build for iOS, iPadOS, macOS, or Android and use the RevenueCat SDK for at least one in-app/web purchase, or serve ads through RevenueCat Ads.
- Except for the student-only Next Gen path, the app's **first public version** must be published during the submission period to Apple's App Store, Google Play, or Samsung Galaxy Store. Previously published apps and mere updates are not eligible.
- Store review can take days, so this contest has a materially larger execution burden than the agent contests.
- Submit text, a public YouTube/Vimeo device demo under two minutes, a 1024×1024 icon, a 1179×2556 screenshot without a device frame, a store URL, and either a free trial or judge promo code for premium features.
- Submission deadline: **September 30, 2026 at 11:45 PM PT**.

## 4. AWS Trainium Frontier — closed

### Credit status

- The official rules state that registered academic and non-academic teams receive AWS Promotional Credits after registration; the credits expire one calendar year after issuance.
- **No dollar amount is published** in the official rules.
- There is no public credit-request form. Credits were tied to acceptance/registration in a competition capped at the first 100 teams.
- The [official Devpost page](https://trainium-frontier.devpost.com/) now says **registration is closed** and that the competition has ended. Therefore no legitimate claim path remains.

Sources: [official rules](https://trainium-frontier.devpost.com/rules), [official Amazon Science announcement](https://www.amazon.science/news/aws-trainium-frontier-competition-co-design-models-and-kernels-on-purpose-built-ai-chips).

### Kazakhstan eligibility

Kazakhstan is absent from the official exclusion list and would have been geographically eligible for an entrant aged 18 or older. This no longer helps because registration is closed.

### Critical build requirements

- GitHub and AWS accounts; team of at most four.
- Use the provided nanochat-derived pipeline and Trainium2 environment; Round 1 requires training within 30 minutes on one `trn2.3xlarge`, producing a valid checkpoint and reproducible end-to-end script with no pretrained weights or external data.
- The top 10 advance to a four-hour full-server Round 2 combining `val_bpb` and CORE evaluation.
- The rules contain one conflicting/stale sentence about Qwen3-30B-A3B NKI inference, while the detailed Round 1 rules and overview describe nanochat training. This ambiguity would need sponsor clarification if registration reopened.

## 5. Agentic Cinema — explicitly unavailable from Kazakhstan

### Eligibility overrides the attractive credits

The [official rules](https://agentic-cinema.devpost.com/rules) explicitly list **Kazakhstan** as excluded. The user must not enter the contest or request contest credits as an eligible participant.

For completeness only, eligible entrants are offered:

- **$100 Google Cloud credit** for an existing GCP account through the [official form](https://forms.gle/XPe837tzogh8L5sX6), requested by **August 31, 2026 at 11:59 PM PST** and generally processed in 1–5 business days.
- ClickHouse advertises **$400 credit** for new accounts through its cloud console.
- Replit advertises a **$20 code** for the first Core month for new users; a payment card is required and the subscription must be cancelled to avoid renewal.
- Grafana has a free tier; IBM Bob/Confluent have trials; Parallel exposes its playground/docs. Their contest resource pages publish no additional fixed promo-credit amount.

Sources: [main resource index](https://agentic-cinema.devpost.com/resources), [ClickHouse resources](https://agentic-cinema.devpost.com/details/clickhouse-resources), [Replit resources](https://agentic-cinema.devpost.com/details/replit-resources), [Grafana resources](https://agentic-cinema.devpost.com/details/grafana-resources), [IBM resources](https://agentic-cinema.devpost.com/details/ibm-resources), [Parallel resources](https://agentic-cinema.devpost.com/details/parallel-resources).

### Critical build requirements (moot for this user)

- A new production-ready agent using Gemini and Google Cloud Agent Builder/ADK plus one chosen partner's product at runtime for a real media/entertainment workflow.
- Only Google Cloud AI and the chosen partner's built-in AI may be used. The Devpost manager states that this restriction also covers development-time coding assistants, not just runtime AI; third-party assistants such as Claude Code are not permitted. See the [official manager clarification](https://agentic-cinema.devpost.com/forum_topics/44644-question-about-the-ai-usage-limitation-grafana-track).
- Hosted web/iOS/Android app, hosted URL, public open-source repository with visible license, and a public demo video of at most three minutes.
